01 / ABOUT

Creative vision.
Technical depth.

I’m Javier Mansilla, a VFX Supervisor based in Barcelona, working across feature films, television, animation and full-CG cinematics. My professional career began in 2000.

My background combines VFX and CG supervision with hands-on experience across the production process. I bring an artistic eye and technical understanding to the work, helping directors turn ambitious ideas into images that serve the story.

Selected projects include Game of Thrones, Halo, John Wick: Chapter 3 – Parabellum, 3 Body Problem and The Mandalorian — Season 1, alongside A Monster Calls, Warcraft, See and Locke & Key. My animation work includes Filmax’s Donkey Xote and Nocturna, alongside Stargate Unleashed and the No Pets Allowed film project.

SCIENTIFIC VISUALISATION · BSC · 2014

Supercomputing and eScience

A documentary for the Barcelona Supercomputing Center exploring how supercomputing advances science and everyday life. Visualisations drawn directly from simulation data reveal research into cancer, supernovas, personalised medicine, wind energy and fluid dynamics.

I created the complete 3D reconstruction of the MareNostrum supercomputer and the chapel that houses it. We then used MareNostrum to render vast scientific datasets with Arnold across thousands of processors, turning previously unseen simulation data into images.

INTERACTIVE VR · MEDIAPRO · 2018

The Zone of Hope

An immersive climate-change experience created for Aigües de Barcelona. Physical and virtual spaces align in a multisensory journey combining interactive environments, 360° imagery and visual effects at 90 frames per second.

Spatial sound and tactile, wind, temperature and humidity effects let visitors feel the consequences of a changing climate. Designed and produced by Mediapro Exhibitions, with an original concept by Mediapro Brands and VR technology by Labsid/MVR.
